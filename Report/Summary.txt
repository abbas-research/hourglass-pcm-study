Objective

Investigate the thermal performance of a volume-matched
Hourglass PCM geometry surrounding a cylindrical 18650
lithium-ion battery.

Simulation Setup

ANSYS Fluent
Transient
Pressure-Based
Laminar
Energy ON
Solidification & Melting ON

Heat Generation = 65000 W/m³
Ambient Temperature = 298.15 K
Convection Coefficient = 7 W/m²K

Time Step = 1 s
Simulation Duration = 1000 s

Mesh Statistics

Nodes = 36,999
Elements = 202,904

Minimum Orthogonal Quality = 0.00963
Average Orthogonal Quality = 0.7636
Maximum Orthogonal Quality = 0.99426

Conclusion

The Hourglass PCM geometry produced an average battery
temperature of 304.164 K and a maximum temperature of
304.508 K.

Compared with the baseline uniform PCM jacket, the
Hourglass geometry increased average temperature by
approximately 2.03 K.
